# Vision Board for Couples 2027

36 shared goals for couples in 6 groups (home, money, travel and adventure, time together, health and energy, family and friends). Each goal has why it matters and a first step you do together this week, plus 12 questions to ask each other and a 45-minute plan for the evening.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/vision-board-for-couples/

- Press a goal and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=couples_2027) as a 1080x1920 phone lock screen with the first step under it; open the same link on the other phone and you both have the same goal
- Or type your own shared goal and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
