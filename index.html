12 questions to ask each other before you choose

Take turns. One person asks, the other answers, then swap. Write down every answer that makes you both nod: those are your goals.

  1. What was your favorite moment of us in 2026?
  2. What do you want to feel more of next year?
  3. What is one thing I could do that would make your week easier?
  4. Where do you want us to be living at the end of 2027?
  5. What is one place you want us to see together?
  6. What does a perfect ordinary Saturday look like for you?
  7. What is one money goal that would make you feel safer?
  8. Which habit of ours do you want to keep, and which one to drop?
  9. What do you want to learn in 2027, and how can I help?
  10. How do you want us to handle the busy weeks?
  11. What tradition do you want us to start?
  12. What is one thing you are proud of us for?

Make it together in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: look back. Each of you names three moments from 2026 you loved and one you want to do differently. Listen, do not fix.
  2. 15 minutes: ask the questions. Take turns with the 12 questions below. Write down every answer that makes you both nod.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three shared goals. One for home or money, one for time together, one just for fun. Fewer goals, kept, beat a long list.
  4. 10 minutes: make it visible. Turn your favorite goal and its first step into the same lock screen on both phones, or make a full board together.

Questions

What is a vision board for couples?

A board of the goals you share as a couple for the coming year, such as a home, a trip, a savings goal or more time together. You make it together, so it doubles as a conversation about what you both want.

How do you make a couples vision board?

Look back at the year together, ask each other a few honest questions, pick three shared goals and make them visible. This page has 36 shared goals with a first step, 12 questions and a 45-minute plan.

What should a couple put on a vision board?

Goals you both care about: where you live, money, travel, time together, health and the people around you. Add one first step to each goal so the board tells you what to do this week, not only what to dream about.

Can we both have the same lock screen?

Yes. Press a goal or type your own, save the 1080x1920 image, then open the same link on the other phone. Seeing the same goal every day is a quiet reminder that you are working on it together.
